session 2026-07-24-wall-65cm
============================

First ground-truthed capture on the built 9.25 x 9.9 cm array, and the
session that exposed the wall reflection problem. Kept as the "before"
half of the room-setup comparison, NOT as sweep data.

Room setup
----------
Array about 65 cm from a wall. Clap about 40 cm from the array, on the
side away from the wall, so clap, array and wall were roughly in line.

Geometry: 9.25x9.9-measured, condition number 1.07.

Result
------
angle000 trial1: estimated 356.6 deg against a true 0, error -3.4 deg.
Channel sync passed at worst 4.10 samples of an 8.32 tolerance, so the
firmware and the array are fine. The error is a room problem.

Why the error
-------------
Image-source method puts the wall echo an extra 2 x 65 cm = 130 cm of
path behind the direct sound, which is 3.79 ms, or 60.6 samples at
16 kHz. The envelope shows a bump at +57 samples at 0.42 of the direct
peak, which matches that prediction to within measurement error on the
wall distance.

That echo lands inside every practical analysis window, and it corrupts
one mic pair badly:

  triangle 0-1-2   residual -0.875 samples   inconsistent
  triangle 1-2-3   residual -0.750 samples   inconsistent
  triangle 0-1-3   residual -0.065 samples   fine
  triangle 0-2-3   residual +0.060 samples   fine

Pair 1-2 reads +3.201 samples where the other five imply +4.076.
Refitting without pair 1-2 gives 359.45 deg, error -0.55 deg, in line
with the simulation's 0.8 to 1.0 deg. Shortening the window to 40
samples, the only length that genuinely excludes the echo, cuts the
worst residual from 0.85 to 0.21 and halves the error.

Do not pool these trials with a sweep recorded further from a wall. The
echo timing is a property of the room, so they are different data.

Also from this session
----------------------
Two analysis bugs found and fixed in commit 0b6517f:
  - check_sync.py SYNC_TOLERANCE was hardcoded at 5.0 samples, sized for
    the retired in-line fixture. The built array's diagonal is 6.32
    samples, so a diagonal clap would have failed correct hardware. Now
    derived from the ACTIVE geometry aperture.
  - find_clap() estimated the noise floor without removing DC. The DFSDM
    offsets summed to 3633 against a measured floor of 3268, so it
    warned "weak transient" on a clap that was about 60 dB over the real
    acoustic noise. SNR now reads 31.1 instead of 11.7.
