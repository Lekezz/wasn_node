Findings
--------
Moving from 65 cm to 2.1 m of wall clearance fixed the reflection that
corrupted pair 1-2 in the 2026-07-24 session.

  session                 bearing error   worst triangle residual
  2026-07-24  wall 65 cm      -3.40 deg           0.875 samples
  2026-07-27  wall 2.1 m      +0.58 deg           0.119 samples

+0.58 deg is at the simulation's own accuracy floor, so at 0 degrees the
array is now as good as the method allows.

All three captures, offline reference:

  trial               true   estimated    error   residual
  angle000/trial1        0      0.58      +0.58     0.119
  angle315/trial1      315    317.12      +2.12     0.347
  angle315/trial2      315    321.38      +6.38     0.499

The triangle residual tracks the error monotonically here, which is what
it is for: it flags a bad capture without needing to know the true angle.
The firmware printed "(inconsistent: suspect a reflection)" on the two
315 degree trials on its own. 315 deg is worse than 0 deg across both
trials, so it is worth re-running that angle and checking what sits on
that diagonal.

Two firmware problems this session exposed, both since fixed:

1. Every float in the board's report printed as blank. The project links
   --specs=nano.specs, whose printf drops floating point unless the link
   also pulls -u _printf_float. Integers printed fine, which is the
   fingerprint. Fixed in localize.c by formatting floats with integer
   arithmetic (f2s), so it cannot break again from a linker setting.

2. TRIGGER_LEVEL 2500 was too high once the wall reflection stopped
   adding energy: the clap peaked at 4444, a margin of only 1.8x, and the
   next trial never triggered. The board kept listening and the capture
   script timed out with no other symptom. Lowered to 1000 against a
   measured noise floor of RMS 10 to 18 per mic.

Partial validation of the port, from the fields that did print: the
board's onset and analysis window matched the Python reference EXACTLY on
all three captures (625/369..2417, 745/489..2537, 534/278..2326). That
covers find_clap including the histogram median approximation the
firmware uses in place of np.median. The float path is still unverified
on hardware, pending a rebuild.
